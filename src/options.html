PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

Consent Intelligence

Deterministic consent detection stays in your browser. When enabled, ambiguous bounded labels may be classified remotely and agreements are analyzed only after you press Analyze.

System One analysis

The API key remains on this server and is never stored in the extension. Your configured System One model handles ambiguous languages.

What the extension does

It runs deterministic checks first, routes only ambiguous summaries to your configured System One classifier, verifies agreement findings against exact evidence, and computes any score from fixed weights.

Limits

Results can be incomplete or wrong and are not legal advice. Partial reviews receive no score. The extension never checks a box, opens an agreement for you, or submits a form.
