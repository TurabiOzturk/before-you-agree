PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

Consent Intelligence

Consent detection stays in your browser. Agreement analysis is optional and sends only the agreement text—not page HTML, form values, browsing history, or your consent decision—to the analysis server you configure.

JEV analysis

The API key remains on this server and is never stored in the extension.

What the extension does

It detects likely agreement controls, retrieves a public linked document or reads an agreement you opened on the page, asks JEV narrow clause questions, verifies evidence, and computes any score from fixed weights.

Limits

Results can be incomplete or wrong and are not legal advice. Partial reviews receive no score. The extension never checks a box, opens an agreement for you, or submits a form.
